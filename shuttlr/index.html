Shuttlr — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 2, 2026

Shuttlr ("the app", "we") helps people organise and join sports games and groups. The app is operated by an individual developer, Kallayil Unnikrishnan Pratheesh Kumar, based in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data Shuttlr handles, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data and the app does not show advertising.

1. Data we collect

DataWhy we need it
Email address, and a password (stored only in hashed form)To create and secure your account, send a one-time verification code, and let you reset your password.
Display name and nicknameTo show who you are to other people in games and groups.
Optional profile photoTo show your picture in the app. Only added if you choose a photo; the app asks for camera or photo-library access only at that moment.
Sports you play and your skill levelsTo recommend suitable games and groups.
Optional mobile numberIf you add one, it is visible only to the platform administrator and is used to contact you about games you were invited to or joined. It is never shown to other users.
Games, groups, invitations and replies you create or take part in (titles, descriptions, venue, time, whether you are going)To run the service you asked for.
Push-notification token, a random device identifier created by the app, and your device platform and app versionTo deliver notifications you opt in to, and to avoid sending the same notification twice to one device.
Technical logs, such as IP address, request time and app versionFor security, abuse prevention and fixing faults.

If you register or sign in with Google (where the app offers it), we receive your name and email address from Google. Shuttlr does not collect your device location, contacts or advertising identifier.

2. How we use your data

We use your data only to provide and secure Shuttlr: running your account, showing games and groups, sending notifications and emails you need, preventing abuse, and handling reports and support requests. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

3. Who can see your data

  • Other users can see your display name, nickname and profile photo where you take part in a game or group, and organisers can see who has joined their games. Who can see a game depends on its visibility setting (public, group, or invite only).
  • Shared links: anyone with a game link can view the game's details (sport, time, venue, capacity, organiser and how many people are going). Names of participants are not shown to people who are not signed in.
  • The administrator can access account data when needed to operate the service, moderate content and respond to reports.
  • We do not share your personal data with advertisers.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run Shuttlr. They process data on our behalf:

  • Google: Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications, Google's mail service sends our emails, and Google Sign-In if you choose it.
  • Hosting provider: our servers and profile-photo storage are currently hosted by Hetzner in Finland. If we change hosting location, we will update this policy.

Shuttlr does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. Because our providers may operate outside Singapore, your data can be transferred internationally. We take reasonable steps to make sure it is protected to a comparable standard.

5. How long we keep data, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen of the app. After you delete it, we delete or anonymise your personal details within a reasonable time. We may keep limited records that do not identify you, or that we must keep for security and legal reasons, such as administrative audit logs. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your name, nickname, photo, sports, skill levels and mobile number in the app.
  • You can switch off notifications in the app or in your device settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to our use of it by emailing us. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the service.
  • To report a problem or content in the app, use "Report a problem or content" in the Profile screen where available, or email us.

We aim to handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only in hashed form, and data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Shuttlr is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this policy first and change the "last updated" date above.
