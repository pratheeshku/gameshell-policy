GameShell — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 17, 2026

GameShell ("the app") is a single-player game app published by an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data collection

GameShell collects no personal data of any kind. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party trackers of any kind embedded in the app.

Network access

GameShell does not use the internet. The app has no networking permission in its Android or iOS build, and makes no network connections — nothing is ever sent from your device.

Nickname and local data

On first launch, the app asks you to pick a nickname. This nickname, your game scores, and your in-progress game are stored only in a file on your own device. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not visible to the developer or any third party. You can permanently erase all of this data at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Reset all data").

Accounts

GameShell has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to communicate with other people through the app.

Children's privacy

GameShell is designed to be suitable for a mixed audience of children and adults. Because the app collects no data of any kind, from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either — no personal information is gathered from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.
